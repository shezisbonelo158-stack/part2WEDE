TITLE: WEBSITE PROPOSAL FOR THE YOUTH DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE   

  

This proposal is for a website created for the Youth Development Initiative, a group dedicated to empowering youth via education and skill development. 

  

The website's goals are to raise awareness, disseminate program details, and draw in volunteers and contributors. It will also operate as a hub for individuals to learn more about the organization's activities and become engaged.  

Young community members, contributors, and prospective volunteers are among the target demographic; therefore, the website needs to be user-friendly and accessible to people from a variety of backgrounds. 

  

An "about us" page description, a blog or news selection, a contribution page, a volunteer registration form, and success stories to motivate visitors and demonstrate the organization's influence are some of the main features. 

The website will promote fundraising initiatives, boost visibility, and enhance communication while bolstering the organization's legitimacy. 

  

Bright, upbeat colors will be used in the website design to represent youth, and its growth layout will be straightforward, mobile-friendly, and captivating.

Typography: 

Because Poppins is contemporary and simple to read, it will be used for headings on the website. Arial or open sans will be used for body content.  

Normal text will utilize a lesser font size, but headings will be bold and larger. 

 

Layout and design: 

This website will have a modern, responsive layout, and the navigation bar will be the same throughout.  

The hero section, impact statistics, programs, about us part, call to action, and contact details will all be included on the homepage. 

 

 

 

 

User experience: 

The layout of this website will make it simple to locate information. HOME, ABOUT, PROGRAMS, DONATE, AND CONTACT will all be navigable. Clear labeling for buttons like "Donate now" and "join us" will be used, and the website will be made to function on PCs, tablets, and smartphones.
